Profile

Physicist (Ph.D., University of Cambridge) turned cleantech entrepreneur, with three decades at DuPont in electronics materials and photovoltaics, including more than a decade as Technical & Marketing Director for the PV segment across EMEA. Through Andromède Consulting SAS, Stephan provides technical services in materials science, product transformation and new applications for the clean-energy industry, and co-founds and invests in companies across the solar value chain: PV asset operation, PV reliability and repair, European PV manufacturing, and battery energy storage.
