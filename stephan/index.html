Profile

Physicist turned business leader with three decades at DuPont, bringing new materials technologies to market across the electronics and solar industries. For more than a decade he led DuPont Photovoltaic Solutions’ marketing and business development across Europe, the Middle East and Africa, with a focus on long-term reliability of solar modules in demanding climates. He is now co-founder and CTO of DOTSun, a French cleantech start-up that repairs, revamps and extends the lifespan of photovoltaic modules on site, and leads Bee Solar’s projects for low-carbon European solar manufacturing and power generation.
