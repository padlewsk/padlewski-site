About

Video producer and editor with more than a decade behind the camera and in the edit suite, from science at CERN and the Solar Impulse round-the-world flight to luxury beauty in London.

Today he heads video production at Dorier Group, creating the content behind conferences, ceremonies and immersive experiences.

Bilingual French–English. Trained at Kingston University (London) in television and video technology, with advanced DaVinci Resolve and After Effects training from SOHO Editors.
